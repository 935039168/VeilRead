Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-16

Local data

TXT content, library metadata, settings, site rules, and reading progress are stored locally in chrome.storage.local and the extension's IndexedDB.

Web page content

Web page text is parsed and displayed on your device only for the reader you can see. Online reading requests occur only after your action and go directly to the same website you selected. VeilRead does not operate a server that receives this data.

What we do not do

The current version contains no advertising, telemetry, third-party analytics, data sale, or cross-site tracking. It does not upload books, reading history, or browsing history to VeilRead.

Deletion and support

When you uninstall the extension, the browser removes its local extension data. You can also clear data through the library or browser extension settings. For support, visit GitHub Issues.
